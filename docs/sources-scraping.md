# Public listing sites for scraping — legality and feasibility (ING-03)

**Status:** draft for lead review · **Checked:** 2026-11-24 · **Author:** benmochen

Research on 8 Montreal public listing sites (downtown and near McGill) to determine, with evidence, which ones AfterClass may legally collect from automatically and which ones it may not.

> **Disclaimer:** This is engineering research, not legal advice. Per ING-03 guidelines, any site whose terms are unclear or silent on automated collection / republication is marked **not allowed**. No automated scraper was run during this research; `robots.txt`, Terms of Use, and top-level listing pages were inspected manually.

## Summary

- **Recommended to scrape right now: 0 of 8 sites.** Six sites explicitly prohibit automated collection, copying, or republication without prior written permission (`mcgill.ca` via `robots.txt` + copyright notice, `mtl.org`, `quartierdesspectacles.com`, `lavitrine.com`, `tastet.ca`, and `ra.co`). Two sites (`montreal.ca/calendrier` web pages and `ssmu.ca/events/`) either reserve all rights on the website or publish no reuse terms, so under our conservative rule they are also **not allowed**.
- **Best non-scraping alternative already exists for city events:** Instead of scraping `montreal.ca/calendrier`, use the **City of Montreal Open Data CSV/GeoJSON** (`vmtl-evenements-publics`, CC BY 4.0, documented in ING-02). Although individual `montreal.ca` event pages include start/end times and Schema.org `Event` JSON-LD currently missing from the CSV, the website itself carries an "all rights reserved" notice; the clean path is requesting those fields from the City's Open Data team.
- **Best partnership / direct-permission targets:** As a McGill student project (GDG McGill), direct email outreach is realistic and far cleaner than scraping for **SSMU** (`info@ssmu.ca`), **McGill Web Services / Channels** (`copyright@mcgill.ca`), **Quartier des Spectacles** (local non-profit cultural partnership), and **La Vitrine** (non-profit cultural hub with a Pro Zone / partner data-sharing program).
- **Personal data warning:** Event pages on **McGill** and **Ville de Montréal** frequently publish individual organizer names, phone numbers, and email addresses. Even if permission is ever granted for any calendar, ingestion must strip personal contact fields and store only venue/event facts.

### Other sites briefly checked (excluded from top 8)

- **Montréal Centre-Ville (`montrealcentreville.ca/evenements/`):** Checked 2026-11-24. Only **12 events** listed (mostly duplicate occurrences of 2 recurring events: Eaton Centre yoga and MAC Place Ville Marie) — too small to justify a scraper. Also publishes no Terms of Use (`/conditions-dutilisation/` is 404; footer asserts `© 2026 Montréal centre-ville. Tous droits réservés.`), so **not allowed** (unclear). Embeds `Event` JSON-LD via WordPress The Events Calendar; direct partnership via `https://montrealcentreville.ca/contact/` is worth considering since they actively target students (*Mon profil → Étudiant*).
- **Time Out Montreal (`timeout.com/montreal`):** Checked 2026-11-24. [Terms of Use](https://www.timeout.com/terms-of-use) §11.2–11.3 explicitly forbid copying, downloading, caching, or using content on any AI/ML system without prior written consent → **not allowed**.

## Technical effort, layout stability, and non-scraping options

Even though **none** of the 8 sites are allowed for unauthorized scraping today, recording their technical structure helps estimate effort **if** written permission is granted later, and highlights cleaner alternatives:

| Site | Rendering & structured data | Layout stability & scraping risk | Recommended non-scraping path |
|---|---|---|---|
| **McGill Events / Channels** | Blocked by **AWS WAF JS challenge** (HTTP `202` on direct GET); Drupal WMS | High risk — WAF blocks simple HTTP clients, `robots.txt` blocks `/*/events/*` | Email McGill Web Services / Communications (`copyright@mcgill.ca` or Campus Life & Engagement) as GDG McGill to request an official JSON/iCal feed |
| **SSMU Events** | Plain server-rendered WordPress HTML (`95 KB`); no `Event` JSON-LD | Medium stability (WordPress theme changes occasionally); very low payoff (~21 governance/giveaway events) | Email `info@ssmu.ca` / VP Student Life for written permission or a live iCal/RSS feed, plus direct club event submissions |
| **Ville de Montréal — Calendrier** | Plain server-rendered HTML + Schema.org `Event` JSON-LD on detail pages | High stability (municipal portal), **but scraping is unnecessary** | Use the official **CC BY 4.0 Open Data CSV/GeoJSON** (`donnees.montreal.ca`); request start/end times in the CSV from the City's Open Data team |
| **Tourisme Montréal (`mtl.org`)** | Heavy client-side JS / dynamic directory loading; no listing JSON-LD | Medium-high effort (needs browser rendering), frequent editorial/UI refreshes | Contact `https://www.mtl.org/en/contact-us` to inquire about a student-app data partnership |
| **Quartier des Spectacles** | Client-side Svelte + Algolia search/filter calendar; no `Event` JSON-LD | Medium-high effort (JS-rendered calendar + Algolia filters disallowed in `robots.txt`) | Contact `https://www.quartierdesspectacles.com/en/contact` — non-profit downtown partnership that may share programming feeds |
| **La Vitrine** | Heavy client-side JS templates; no `Event` JSON-LD on homepage | High effort & medium stability | Reach out via `https://www.lavitrine.com/en/contact` or **Pro Zone** (`https://pro.lavitrine.com/en`) about their data-sharing / partnership program |
| **Tastet** | Plain WordPress HTML + RSS/WP-JSON | Low technical effort, **high legal risk** (explicit anti-bot clause §II.L) | Commercial licensing only (Tastet monetizes its directory via Tastet+); skip |
| **Resident Advisor Montreal** | JS-rendered SPA behind **anti-bot protection (HTTP 403)** | Very high risk & effort (explicit scraping ban + active blocking) | Do not scrape; partner directly with local venues/promoters or use a licensed event API (ING-01) |

## Sites recommended for scraping

**None (0 sites).**

We do **not** recommend building a web scraper against any of the 8 Montreal public listing sites reviewed:

1. **Clear legal blocks:** 6 of 8 sites (`mcgill.ca`, `mtl.org`, `quartierdesspectacles.com`, `lavitrine.com`, `tastet.ca`, `ra.co`) explicitly prohibit automated collection, downloading, or republication without prior written consent (and `mcgill.ca` / `ra.co` also block requests technically via `robots.txt`, WAF, or HTTP 403).
2. **Conservative rule on silent/unclear sites:** `ssmu.ca/events/` and `montreal.ca/calendrier` do not grant public-app reuse on their web pages; marking them **not allowed** avoids legal risk for little gain (`ssmu.ca` only has ~21 mostly governance events, and `montreal.ca` already publishes an open dataset).
3. **Cleaner paths for ING-04:**
   - **Already usable with zero scraping:** City of Montreal Open Data (`CC BY 4.0`, ING-02) + OpenStreetMap / Overture Maps (ING-02).
   - **Highest-value outreach targets (ask for written permission / direct feed):**
     1. **SSMU (`info@ssmu.ca`) & McGill Channels** — directly solves the student-event gap identified in ING-02.
     2. **Quartier des Spectacles & La Vitrine** — both are Montreal cultural non-profits right next to campus with strong downtown event coverage.
     3. **Tourisme Montréal** — covers downtown venues, bars, and major festivals if they are open to a McGill student partnership.
   - **For nightlife/clubs and reliable hours/prices:** Scraping is not a legal substitute; evaluate paid/licensed APIs in **ING-01** or direct organizer/student submissions.
